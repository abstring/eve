# Artwork inspiration and image sources

Verified 2026-10-05.

- **Artist:** Peter Paul Rubens (1577–1640)
- **Artwork:** *Adam and Eve*, 1598–1600
- **Medium:** Oil on panel
- **Collection:** City of Antwerp Collection, Rubenshuis, Antwerp, Belgium

## Authoritative museum record

[Rubenshuis: Peter Paul Rubens, Adam and Eve](https://www.rubenshuis.be/en/peter-paul-rubens-adam-and-eve)
identifies the artwork, collection, date, and medium. The museum labels its image
**public domain** and provides a Download link. It currently lists the painting
as **in storage**, so collection attribution should not imply it is on display.

[Museum image download](https://assets.rubenshuis.be/styles/free/public/2026-01/DIG29376-s1JPW9bniYTaUWFBjQpzs2Kz.jpg?VersionId=.xXzeV1fb8szQju6OCJB2N9eOTGycBay&itok=J3avi0Ih).
Prefer the museum record as the durable citation; the asset URL may change.

## Open reproduction with photographer credit

The [Flemish Art Collection feature](https://vlaamsekunstcollectie.be/en/news/peter-paul-rubens-adam-en-eve-rubenshuis)
publishes full and detail reproductions, labelled **CC0**, with photography
credited to **Ans Brys**. That credit belongs to those reproductions; it is not
a verified photographer credit for the initial reference crop used for Eve.

## Initial reference and logo adaptation

The user supplied a detail of Eve and identified its source as
[PeterPaulRubens.net](https://www.peterpaulrubens.net/adam-and-eve.jsp).
That site is not the museum's collection record. The exact photographic
provenance of that supplied crop has not been established.

The logo was generated from that visual reference and then adapted to show Eve
inspecting a PCB, framed by natural leaves. It is a new engraved-style
interpretation, not an unmodified museum reproduction. The approved light logo,
dark edition, and icon masters are documented in the brand package.

Suggested credit:

> public domain. Adapted for Eve to depict PCB inspection.
